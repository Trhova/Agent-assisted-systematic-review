# Primary-linked publisher supplement recovery v1

Normal supplement acquisition may try the Springer content host after the primary link and Europe PMC archive fail. This is limited to a verified primary DOI matching the bibliographic record, a 10.1007/s journal identifier, and the exact original linked MOESM filename whose journal/year/article identifiers match that DOI. The URL is a DOI/filename-derived route, not falsely labelled an extracted publisher link. Failed routes remain in the attachment audit. Existing bounded request/time limits apply; a response body must pass the ordinary parser and context/provenance checks. No guessed alternate article assets or cross-study files are admitted.

A separately versioned upgrade accepts a preserved successful HTTP receipt and identical bytes only for that primary-derived URL, then replays normal augment() in cache-only mode into a new source directory. All original source pins remain unchanged. Access recovery does not imply that the requested outcome exists, establish analyzed outcome denominators, or authorize a numerical row.

The single-source continuation uses the unchanged frozen scientific outcome contract and the augmented primary source, with separate extraction and verification requests. It exports new extraction/availability artifacts without replacing prior trial decisions, accepting numeric rows, conducting family adjudication or pooling. Eligibility and missing outcome data remain distinct. Negative findings concern the supplied reports, not undiscovered companion reports. Same-model verification may share errors. New numerical candidates require the normal scientific gates in a separately versioned review continuation.
