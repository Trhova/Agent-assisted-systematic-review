# Exact known-status binary complement v1

15October2026. Opt-in only; original reported-count protocols unchanged. Cochrane Handbook6.1.1 and6.4 define a dichotomous response with two categories and require per-group event totals and denominators. Chapter6.4 discusses the material effect of event direction on risk ratios: reversing events is not taking the reciprocal of an RR. Source: https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-06 .

When a primary report explicitly establishes all analyzed participants have one of two exhaustive outcome states at a given time, target events equal known-status N minus reported opposite events. Preserve both source integers, population, time, event definitions, exact quotations, source hash and deterministic transformation. No percentage reconstruction, guessed denominator, inferred missing status, incompatible timepoints or overlapping categories. The schema obtains originals; deterministic derivation rejects incompatible counts and records the audit. Normal full-source verification must establish semantics and denominator support; arithmetic/exact quotes alone are insufficient. A failed source check remains unresolved. This policy cannot make heterogeneous endpoints equivalent.
