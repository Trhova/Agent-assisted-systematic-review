# Additional historical development case — 14 October 2026

The selected case is Pérez-Gómez et al. 2020, *Effects of Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) on VO2max*, DOI [10.3390/nu12041119](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7230697/). Selection was based on accessible primary reports, individually randomized parallel designs, and the published endpoint MD/random-effects estimator supported by the runtime. It was not selected by expected numerical agreement. All four published quantitative contributors and the fifth qualitative report were acquired before execution. This is a historical development case inspected during qualification, not held-out validation.

The scope has genuine primary-evidence problems, despite access qualification. Choudhary's abstract and results describe 24 analyzed Ashwagandha participants and 25 placebo, while the numerical table headings reverse these denominators. Shenoy explicitly reports 18/19 analyzed after withdrawals, while the review reports 20/20. Tripathi randomizes two active doses and assesses day 14 and final day 28; the review describes only 330 mg and two weeks. Sandhu does not clearly establish the measured units/maximal-exercise construct; an unusually low numerical value is not itself a defensible exclusion. These problems must be surfaced by extraction and comparison rather than silently repaired or used to force a match.

The prospectively frozen execution contract uses final on-treatment endpoints and combines all eligible disjoint dose arms, following Cochrane Handbook sections 6.2.4, 6.2.9 and 6.5.2.10. It retains unresolved contradictory denominators/units. This is a source-led comparison with the published review, not a promise of literal reproduction of its dose/time choices. Any numerical match requires a separate dose/timepoint and source-value audit before interpretation. Pooling uses the existing DL random-effects normal-CI MD implementation; no median or SE conversion is introduced for this case.

Primary-source acquisition is assisted. MED reports are from original PMC JATS and licensed PMC Cloud PDFs. Tripathi is the original open publisher PDF. Malik has no assigned DOI identified; an author-posted original report was retrieved through the web tool and its complete article text selected from lines 49–790, excluding subsequent platform recommendations and later reviews. Both raw tool captures, exact line bounds, source URL, original article title and hashes are preserved. The local direct journal request returned an institutional blocking page, not a PDF; its failed bytes remain in the qualification directory but are not used as evidence. The journal's current reformatted copy also changes pagination/issue labels, so the author-posted original is used.

The case exposed two concrete acquisition defects now repaired: bibliographic supplements had required a DOI even for legitimate reports without one; and no reusable importer preserved line-numbered web primary evidence. `scripts/import_source_capture.py` now rejects gaps, conflicting overlaps, missing source identity and nonempty destinations, while retaining the raw captures. It does not perform scientific screening or extraction. Its inputs are actual source captures, never generated replacement article text.

Frozen inputs: `benchmarks/protocols/perez2020_vo2max_v1.yaml`, `benchmarks/references/perez2020_vo2max_v1.json`, `benchmarks/sources/perez2020_bibliographic_leads_v1.json`, `benchmarks/sources/perez2020_qualification_v1.json`, and `benchmarks/suite_perez2020_v1.yaml`. Pre-execution model/code/source configuration: `data/benchmarks/perez2020_v1/preregistration.json`. Only original source files were seeded; screening and extraction requests are fresh and do not receive reference pooled answers.

## Observed scope repair

V1 finished with seven screened records, five original reports read, one usable comparison and four unresolved candidates. No synthesis was possible. The integrity audit passed and the reproduction gate failed. The model additionally rejected named exercise prediction methods because v1 had unnecessarily required an explicit validation claim within each primary trial. That requirement was not present in the published review's eligibility criteria. Cooper's original 1968 validation article is preserved as `Cooper1968_public_copy.pdf` (a second attempted institutional URL returned HTTP403 and is not used).

V2 separately freezes the corrected published scope: named exercise prediction tests or stated VO₂max prediction formulas are eligible without requiring the intervention trial to redo method validation. Measurement validity remains a limitation. The final-timepoint, all-eligible-dose, denominator, unit and source-integrity requirements stay fixed. Protocol/source/model configuration was frozen before fresh v2 model requests. The original failed v1 remains unchanged. V2 command substitutes `suite_perez2020_v2.yaml` and `data/benchmarks/perez2020_v2` in the command above.

Separate evaluator-only forest transcription is saved in `benchmarks/findings/perez2020_reference_reading_v1.json`. Its four displayed comparisons produce MD 2.99828 [0.17606, 5.82049], consistent with the published 3.00 [0.18, 5.82]. This verifies arithmetic compatibility only. Direct figure inspection also shows that Choudhary contributes the interim eight-week values, not the twelve-week end-of-treatment values. The evaluator reading and its numerics are never supplied to extraction requests.
