# Accessible development benchmark selection, 13 October 2026

The additional benchmark is the hospital-length-of-stay outcome of Rawat et al., *Vitamin C and COVID-19 treatment: A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials*, DOI [10.1016/j.dsx.2021.102324](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dsx.2021.102324). This is a historical methodological reproduction, not current clinical advice or held-out validation. Candidate/reference sources were inspected before protocol registration; the extraction backend receives the primary article and protocol, not the reference estimates.

Selection was based on access and methodological fit: four independent parallel comparisons; public primary full texts for all four; a supported inverse-variance random-effects endpoint SMD; three reported mean/SD summaries and one explicitly reported median/IQR summary. Hakamifard's publisher PDF states first online publication on 14 April 2021 although its final issue citation is 2022. Its randomization description uses odd/even assignment of computer-generated random numbers; its risk assessment must be based on that primary description, not copied from the review's judgment.

Primary sources checked before model execution:

- Zhang, PMID 33420963, [PMC7794643](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7794643/): full JATS, hospital stay in Table 2.
- Kumari, PMID 33409026, [PMC7779177](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7779177/): full JATS, Table 2.
- Siahkali, PMID 33573699, [PMC7877333](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7877333/): full JATS, outcome quartiles and group denominators.
- Hakamifard, [publisher PDF](https://www.immunopathol.com/PDF/ipp-8-e8.pdf): complete six-page article, flow diagram and narrative hospital-stay result. A frozen bibliographic-only backward-citation lead passes through ordinary screening, source identity checking and independent extraction verification.

Raw downloads and source hashes are retained in `data/benchmarks/candidate_review_sources/` and `data/benchmarks/rawat2021_v1/preregistration.json`. The reference review's Figure 2c is retained as an evaluation source only. The historical target covers four hospital-stay contributors; the review's six reports across all outcomes must not be conflated with four independent hospital-stay comparisons.

The optional summary estimator uses Luo et al. equation 11 for mean and Wan et al. equation 16 for SD, the quartile-only case of the Luo/Wan/Shi framework. Both primary method papers were retrieved; the implementation also agrees with the independent example in [metafor's documentation](https://wviechtb.github.io/metafor/reference/conv.fivenum.html). Estimated summaries retain a normal-distribution assumption, original values, exact supporting quotes, analyzed n, source locator/hash and transformation identity. Conventional effect variances omit additional summary-estimation uncertainty. A mandatory sensitivity excludes estimated summaries; it is not compared as though it were the complete published target. No intermediate rounding is tuned to the reference forest.

Other candidates were rejected before execution because their intended comparisons required unsupported crossover/dependence handling, inaccessible sources, retrospective correlation imputation, or a different statistical estimator: early beetroot trials, honey reviews, melatonin reviews, oil-pulling reviews, and broader internet-CBT reviews. PMC11528811 was flagged as retracted and was not used. Failed access attempts and downloaded candidate evidence are preserved. Rawat was not selected on whether its numerical estimate was likely to match.

The MED search is a transparent accessible translation, augmented by documented hand-search leads; it does not reproduce the original Embase/Scopus search or unpublished author correspondence. The protocol was frozen at 17:04 UTC before numerical model execution. New results belong to this development version, leaving all earlier failed reviews, protocols and comparisons intact.

## Observed broader-case limitation and compatible published subgroup

The broader execution did **not** establish a four-trial reproduction. After generic quote and mixed-layout PDF repairs, the verifier could recover Hakamifard's denominator but found that the hospital-stay `±` statistic is not defined as SD or SE. Baseline-table SD labels do not establish the dispersion type of a different outcome. The trial remains unpooled; the review's interpretation was not copied into primary extraction.

A newly retrieved quercetin/vitamin-C/bromelain report (PMID 34803451) also remains unresolved in the broader question: follow-up ends at discharge, intubation or death, its total-hospitalization endpoint and analyzed denominators are uncertain, and it reports min/max rather than quartiles. Its MED creation date (22 November 2021) and Europe PMC first-index date (30 November 2021) follow the review's 18 September cutoff despite an August publication date. This explains a plausible historical MED coverage difference, not its status in every database. The frozen current-index query retains it.

For a method-compatible comparison alongside that failure, a **separate post-hoc development protocol** targets the review's published intravenous-administration subgroup (Figure 7b, three trials). This is a published clinical subgroup, not a newly computed three-trial reference invented from our outputs. The IV protocol excludes oral regimens, requires source-extracted route agreement, retains the same outcome/estimator/conversion policy, and adds the review's other non-MED backward-citation lead for screening. Its protocol, model, code, reference and tolerance were frozen before a fresh screening/extraction execution. Only the identical-query raw bibliographic snapshot and primary documents were seeded; no model decisions or compiled effects were copied. This is not an independent held-out test: the broad-case evidence and observed statistics were already known during subgroup selection.

Source-audit correction: this report was initially described as oral from context. Its primary methods and the retrieved NCT04377789 registry identify supplementation but do not explicitly state the vitamin-C route. The IV run therefore retains route uncertainty. The frozen IV protocol’s explanatory version note contains that earlier contextual label; it is preserved as an audit artifact, not treated as route evidence or an exclusion rule. The operative eligibility rule requires route confirmation from primary methods.
